# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-06-16
**App:** Stickered (iOS)
**Contact:** ljzhe96@gmail.com

## Summary

Stickered does not upload, transmit, or store your photos on any
server. All image processing happens on your device. We do not
collect personal information, do not use accounts, and do not run
analytics or advertising.

## What we access on your device

- **Camera** — used only when you tap *Capture* to take a new photo
  inside the app. Photos are processed locally on your device.
- **Photo library (read)** — used only when you tap *Import* to pick
  an existing photo. We read only the photo you choose.
- **Photo library (write)** — used only when you tap *Save* to write
  a finished Sticker back to your device's photo library.

We do not access your camera, microphone, or photo library in the
background. We do not record audio or video.

## What we collect

Nothing. Stickered has no accounts, no login, and no server. Photos,
extracted subjects, and finished Stickers are stored only on your
device. The on-device Library is not synced to any cloud.

## Third parties

Stickered uses Apple's on-device Vision framework
(`VNGenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest`) to extract subjects from
your photos. This runs entirely on your device. No image data is
sent to Apple or to us.

We do not use third-party analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs.

## Children

Stickered is a general-audience photo tool. It does not knowingly
collect any information from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the effective date above
will be updated. Continued use of Stickered after a change means you
accept the updated policy.
