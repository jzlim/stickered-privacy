# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** 2026-10-31
**App:** Stickered (iOS)
**Contact:** ljzhe96@gmail.com

## Summary

Stickered never uploads, transmits, or stores your photos on any server. All image processing
happens on your device. There are no accounts and no advertising. To decide what to improve,
the app sends a small number of **anonymous usage counts** (for example "a sticker was saved") —
never your photos, your stickers, or anything you type. You can turn this off in Settings.

## What we access on your device

- **Camera** — used only when you tap Capture to take a new photo inside the app. Photos are
  processed locally on your device.
- **Photo library (read)** — used only when you tap Import to pick an existing photo. We read
  only the photo you choose.
- **Photo library (write)** — used only when you tap Save to write a finished Sticker back to
  your device's photo library.

We do not access your camera, microphone, or photo library in the background. We do not record
audio or video.

## Your photos and Stickers

Photos, extracted subjects, finished Stickers, and Scenes are stored only on your device, in the
app's Library. They are never sent to us or to any server. Like the rest of your apps' data, the
Library is included in your device's own iCloud or computer backup if you have backups turned on —
that backup is managed by Apple and your device, not by us, and we cannot see it.

## Anonymous usage stats

To learn which features people use and where the app falls short, Stickered sends anonymous
usage events to **TelemetryDeck**, a privacy-focused analytics service based in Germany.

**What is sent:** the name of an action and a few fixed details about it — for example "sticker
saved" (first save or not, which outline style), "photo chosen" (camera or import), "paywall
shown" (which feature opened it), and "purchase completed" — plus basic technical context such
as the app version, iOS version, device model, and your device's language and region setting.

**What is never sent:** your photos, your stickers or Scenes, any text you type, colours you
pick, file names, your name, email, or GPS location, and no advertising identifier.

**How it stays anonymous:** to count unique users, the app creates a random identifier that is
hashed (scrambled one-way) on your device before it is sent, and hashed again by TelemetryDeck.
Neither TelemetryDeck nor we can link it back to you or to any other app. TelemetryDeck does not
store IP addresses. This data is not used for tracking or advertising and is not sold.

**Turning it off:** open **Settings → Share anonymous usage stats** in the app and switch it off.
From then on, nothing is sent.

## Purchases

Stickered Pro is a one-time purchase handled entirely by Apple through the App Store. We never
see your payment details or Apple ID. The app checks your purchase on the device using Apple's
StoreKit. Whether a purchase was completed is included in the anonymous usage stats above.

## Third parties

- **Apple Vision** (`VNGenerateForegroundInstanceMaskRequest`) extracts subjects from your photos.
  It runs entirely on your device; no image data is sent to Apple or to us.
- **Apple StoreKit** handles the Stickered Pro purchase (see Purchases).
- **TelemetryDeck** receives the anonymous usage stats described above
  ([TelemetryDeck privacy policy](https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/)).

We do not use advertising or tracking SDKs.

## Children

Stickered is a general-audience photo tool. It does not knowingly collect personal information
from anyone, including children under 13.

## Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a meaningful way, the effective date above will be updated. Continued
use of Stickered after a change means you accept the updated policy.
